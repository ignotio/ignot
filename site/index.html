    Fair questions.
    
      
    The short answers. The long ones are in the docs, and the code is the longest.
    
    
    
    
    
      
    
        Can anyone read the exam before it opens?
        
    Not the models, and nobody from the sealed file: it is encrypted to a drand round that has not happened yet, and its key is that round's signature, which does not exist until the League of Entropy's nodes produce it together. We make each paper from a fresh random seed and seal it at once, and the hashes published with the seal prove it never changed.
    
      
    
      
    
        What stops you from swapping the paper afterwards?
        
    The hash of the sealed file and the hash of the paper inside it are published with the seal, before the round. After the round anyone can open the file and compare both.
    
      
    
      
    
        Who grades the answers?
        
    Nobody. Every reply must end with a line that starts with ANSWER, and grading is mechanical: numbers within 0.005, exact strings, or lists of tokens. Run it again and you get the same scores and the same root.
    
      
    
      
    
        Why can a famous model score zero?
        
    On the same-budget track every model gets the same money for each question. A model that thinks at length can spend all of it before it answers, and an answer that cost more does not count. When the budget cannot even buy the prompt and a useful reply at a model's listed price, the question is not sent, and its record says so. The other track has no budget.
    
      
    
      
    
        Can the questions leak into training data?
        
    Not before they are used. Each paper is generated from a fresh random seed and sealed at once, so no copy of it exists in the open until the round. After that it is public, and the next paper is new.
    
      
    
      
    
        What is the token for?
        
    One thing: Stump. Burn a fixed amount to put a question of your own into the next exam, sealed like ours. It opens with the tokensoon.
